A LITTLE BETTER, EVERY GENERATION.

Learning by going
a little further.

Every car starts with a random brain: 8 inputs, 2 actions, and your choice of 1–4 hidden layers (1–24 neurons each). The default is 8 → 12 → 2. Seven rays measure walls and barriers; one input measures speed. The network chooses steering and acceleration. Physics includes acceleration, braking, drag and speed-dependent steering; tire slip and traction loss are not modeled.

  1. Try. Every driver in diverse mode runs the same 4–6 scenarios sequentially, with fresh physics and unchanged weights in each attempt. Selection waits for the entire battery. Fixed mode evaluates only the open environment. Choose 1–10 laps per attempt (default: 3). Cars keep moving across the finish line. Walls, barriers, stalling, or a budget of 34 simulated seconds per target lap end each attempt.
  2. Keep. Ordered, directional checkpoints reward forward progress. Reversing and repeating ground earn nothing. Progress accumulates across all ordered laps. Completing the target wins; a faster total time breaks ties. Diverse mode ranks scenario completion rate, mean progress, worst-case progress and distance-normalized time. It has no single-lap bonus. Fixed mode retains the original fast-lap bonus.
  3. Change. The top 4% survive unchanged. In diverse mode, all elites and parents use the aggregate ranking. Reserved tests never affect selection. Tournament selection and random weight mutations create the rest. Then everyone starts again.

Training runs in a Web Worker at a fixed 60 Hz. Pace adds ticks without changing physics. Saved drivers keep their original architecture and weights. Replay original restores the selected source scenario; Test current environment preserves the open layout. Importing a brain also preserves the open environment. Changing the architecture starts a fresh population; evolution settings join the next generation. Changing the lap target keeps the population and resets scores/history for the new goal. Champions keep their original lap target and obstacle layout, including legacy clear-track files. Edit obstacles to add, drag, rotate or remove five obstacle shapes while training is paused; applying a layout in diverse mode changes the current transfer environment while preserving the fixed training battery and its scores. Fixed mode restarts attempts and scores while keeping the population.

Drive with WASD or arrows. Space pauses. R restarts your manual run. Hold focus on the track; the same physics applies to everyone.
